# v0.13A11 — Frame One Visual Lesson Language Pilot

## Design language

**Show → Compare → Notice → Explain → Try.** The existing full lesson text is
still there, but it is no longer the first experience for the pilot lessons.

The shared `VisualLessonPilot` renderer appears immediately after the existing
lesson header and *before* the main article. Learners see an original, large
illustrated 16:9 study, switch between two deliberately different filmmaking
choices, notice a specific change, optionally reveal the director/editor/sound
designer's reasoning, and open a real Creative Lab. Existing paragraphs become
an opt-in **Explore the craft in more detail** section.

The new visual UI's own format is consistent: a short cinematic question,
illustrated A/B stage, two big keyboard/phone-operable switches, a Notice
panel, expandable reasons, a single memorable takeaway and an actual phone
experiment. No new account, network media, platform permission, storage key,
progress shortcut, autoplay or video upload.

## Three initial lessons, three honest experiments

| Existing lesson | The actual visible change | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| **lighting** | An original vector cup in a fixed room is drawn with soft front lighting vs stronger, directional side lighting, highlight and shadow | An illustrative lighting comparison, **not** a realistic renderer |
| **rhythm** | One 6-second wide framing becomes three different 2-second storyboard framings, including hand and cup inserts; duration labels and filmstrip physically change | A **static editing plan**, no fake playback or fabricated viewer data |
| **ambience** | One identical illustrated room stays locked while imagined indoor quiet changes to written fan and traffic sound layers | **Written sound directions, not audio**. The real Sound Lab has separate original sound sketches |

Tone is deliberately non-dogmatic. The app says what changes visually or
what sounds are *imagined*, not what every viewer must feel. All artwork
is authored locally as CSS and inline SVG, without licensed movie stills.

## Content and compatibility

- All 33 original lessons still exist and use the exact original routes.
- The pilot keeps its existing quiz, completion and local practice notes.
- The 33 Telugu helper summaries remain available through the original toggle.
- Non-pilot lesson pages remain unchanged, including their ordinary paragraphs.
- Lesson progress, private gallery, one saved Make Lab storyboard, previous
  navigation logic, backups and the seven Creative Labs are untouched.
- This is a **visual system pilot**, not a claim to have rewritten the other
  30 lessons or built professional film simulation.

## QA

- Static source check verifies the 3 real source lesson IDs, authoring
  completeness, correct timings and qualified sound/illustration claims,
  accessible A/B controls and the preserved lesson storage/completion path.
- Real Chromium browser QA opens all three pilot lessons, checks that light
  and rhythm illustrations actually differ between selected takes, confirms
  that the sound illustration remains identical while labels change, and
  validates the original lesson text and quiz still appear.
- Real 390px and 320px browser QA checks image responsiveness, reveals
  reasons and measures any horizontal overflow, with screenshots.
- Standard full TypeScript/Vite/PWA builds, Android debug packaging and
  existing lesson/backup/emulator tests remain enabled.

## Android distribution warning

This remains a source-first change. A CI-built debug APK is **temporarily
signed** and cannot be assumed to update a prior installation without data
loss. The stable preview release remains **v0.12A2** until persistent QA
signing and human real-phone upgrade/backup acceptance are verified.
