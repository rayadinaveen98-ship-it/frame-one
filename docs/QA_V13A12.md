# v0.13A12 — Visual filmmaking language: framing, composition & colour

## What students actually see
Frame One now offers six visual-first lessons, including three newly illustrated, interactive comparisons:
- **Shot size**: a room/character wide shot versus a closer action detail. The visible environment changes with the framing; this is a story information comparison, not a simulation of lens optics.
- **Composition**: the same-size illustrated character shifts from centre to the left third against an identical background, revealing more negative space.
- **Colour**: an otherwise identical illustrated still-life changes a cup from a muted neutral tone to a warm red accent. No claim that red always conveys a particular emotion.

Each lesson opens with a large 16:9 original SVG frame, two A/B buttons with real artwork changes, a specific observation, a revealable filmmaking explanation, one memorable takeaway, and the *existing* Frame/Colour Lab. Full original reading stays available under the native collapsed **Explore the craft in more detail** disclosure.

This is an expansion of the previously working three-lesson prototype (Lighting, Editing Rhythm and Ambience), **not** a conversion of every lesson. All 33 source lessons, quizzes, practice notes, Telugu helpers, completion, personal frames, offline behavior and legacy JSON backup keys remain unchanged.

## Real QA
- Source contract checks all six authored examples and the three newly implemented original illustrations.
- Real desktop browser automation opens all three additions, switches A/B, requires the SVG actually changes, reveals the reasoning, checks its quiz and saved-note editor, and captures screenshots.
- Real 390px and 320px browser automation repeats the comparisons and checks no horizontal overflow.
- Full TypeScript/Vite, offline asset generation, seven original Labs and Android debug build are required before handoff.

## Safety and release
All new art is bundled original source; no movie footage, hosted assets, accounts, new permissions, new persistence or recording features. Its debug APK will be temporary-signed. A temporary signer **cannot be assumed to upgrade any previously installed APK**. The publicly managed preview remains v0.12A2 pending persistent signing and real-device upgrade acceptance.
