# Frame One v0.15A7 — Direct & Shoot: two new phone missions

## Experience
Expand the existing Film it yourself Practice Desk from four to six real, filmable three-step missions, rather than creating a new oversized Home row. The desk remains collapsed by default, and the original four exercises remain unchanged.

**One action. Two intentions.** Record the same hand reaching for an envelope in two versions: a decisive reach and a reach with a hesitation. Learners can use their own hand, one phone, one table and paper. This is about controlling intention and performance without dialogue, not guaranteeing an emotion.

**One subject. Two viewpoints.** Film a cup and notebook from a safely held overhead viewpoint and then near cup height while holding the set constant. Compare which information is visible and which object becomes prominent. This is camera height and composition, not a lens/optics simulation.

Both new exercises have an original, interactive A/B SVG storyboard above the three real-world shooting steps. Buttons change the actual drawing, visible explanatory note and accessible label. On changing exercises, the storyboard resets to Take A. There is no simulated camera feed, video playback or fabricated scoring.

## Existing services
- Uses unchanged session-only three-step checkmarks, 240-character reflection, pocket shoot-plan copy/manual fallback, and existing route-to-Lab and route-to-lesson controls.
- Lesson handoffs now include acting and character → acting direction; camera → camera height. Original 15 lesson handoffs remain unchanged (18 total).
- Retain the old 33 lesson IDs, seven labs, four original exercises, learning keys, backed-up projects and backup version.
