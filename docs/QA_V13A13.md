# Frame One v0.13A13 — Learn cinema as a three-beat story

The v0.13A11–A12 A/B illustrations taught students to **compare choices**.
This milestone adds a complementary **reveal the scene one shot at a time**
format to stop lessons feeling like reading an encyclopedia. Each illustration
is original, bundled offline and moves the story forward only when tapped.

## Three authored lessons

| Existing lesson | Sequence | What the viewer discovers |
|---|---|---|
| `units` — Frame, shot, scene | Single still → three moments within one shot → three planned views of one story action | Frame = one picture; shot = uninterrupted view; scene = a story event. Drawings are not simulated playback. |
| `showdonttell` — Show a feeling through action | Set two cups → hesitate near one → put one cup away | Visible choices give clues, not a mandated emotion or ban on dialogue. |
| `continuity` — Make separate shots feel connected | Cup begins on left → hand approaches → cup inexplicably appears on right | A prop-position mismatch across an edit can distract unless deliberate. |

The student sees a large 16:9 storyboard, taps **Reveal next shot** or a
three-beat selector, notices a concise caption, then optionally unlocks the
film-craft explanation **only after viewing the final beat**. They can return
and replay without changing completion or claiming they actually filmed it.
Each links to its existing creative Lab and a real-world small exercise.

The normal reading sections remain under an opt-in disclosure. For
`units`, its prior three-panel legacy storyboard is inside that disclosure
as optional supplemental material rather than duplicating the new artwork
on the main screen.

## Compatibility and QA

- Total visual-first lessons: **9 of 33** (six A/B examples, three scenes).
- Existing 33 quizzes, Telugu helpers, notes, Make Lab storyboard, personal
  gallery, seven Labs and local backup schema stay unchanged.
- No new local keys, cloud uploads, video files, runtime permissions,
  external illustration assets or subscriptions.
- Test source model against real curriculum IDs, story beat accuracy,
  accessible controls, optional insight and original quiz/progress pathways.
- Real browser exercises all three stages of every story, verifies
  three different SVG images, tests discovery hiding on re-entry,
  preserves original notes and quiz, and captures desktop/320px/390px
  screenshots without overflow.
- Android debug build is a **test APK only**. The public preview remains
  v0.12A2 until persistent signing and real-device upgrade acceptance.
  A temporary-signed APK cannot be presumed update-safe over old installs.
