# Content accuracy and editorial policy

**Draft, not educator-approved.** Every passage should be technically correct and very simple for complete beginners. Use ordinary language before specialist definitions. Distinguish frame/shot/take/scene, music/ambience/silence, storyboard illustration/actual filmed footage. Explain that lighting, shot sizes, composition, colour and editing change information/emphasis but do not universally guarantee particular emotions.

The Indian 24-craft framework is an industry-context concept, not a global fixed enumeration of every production role. Explain historical disputes rather than declaring contested "first films" as uncontested facts.
