# Frame One v0.15A8 — camera decisions, not camera theory walls

## Why
The Camera, Movement and Lenses lessons already use two authored A/B drawings to explain basic principles. This extension adds a small optional hands-on decision exercise **below their existing study** to let learners choose a third possibility and reflect on why it changes the shot.

## Three lessons
- Camera / camera height: eye level, lower view, higher view; compare floor, ceiling and vertical relationship.
- Movement: pan from one point, sideways camera translation (track), push toward subject; contrast rotation, parallax and changing camera distance.
- Lenses / viewpoint: fixed wide frame, cropped frame from the same position, physically move closer; contrast field of view and perspective.

Every choice updates an **original, accessible SVG concept diagram**, a plain-language observation, and an on-demand director explanation with a phone filming suggestion. It is not a lens simulator or film capture tool; illustrations are intentionally simplified and make no calibrated optical or emotional claims.

## Experience and safeguards
- Optional native disclosure, closed by default. No additional Home banners, levels, scores, data collection or lesson-completion shortcuts.
- Reuses the three existing routes and all original A/B pilots and lesson text; original lessons, quizzes, Telugu helpers, learner notes, backup format and seven labs remain unchanged.
- No permissions, network requests, camera recording, external media, storage or retained state.
